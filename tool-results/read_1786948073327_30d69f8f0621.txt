   407→## 6. Cross-Team Collaboration in Defense/Aerospace Product Development
   408→
   409→### Work Breakdown Structures (WBS) in Defense
   410→
   411→A **Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)** is defined by the **Defense Acquisition University (DAU)** as:
   412→> "A product-oriented family tree of hardware, software, services, data, and facilities."
   413→
   414→#### Key Characteristics:
   415→- **Product-oriented decomposition** — not activity-oriented
   416→- Hierarchical format breaking complex systems into manageable components
   417→- Relates elements of work to each other and to the end product
   418→- Used for cost estimation, scheduling, risk management, and performance tracking
   419→- Standardized across defense programs (MIL-STD-881 in the US, similar standards internationally)
   420→
   421→#### WBS Levels in Defense Product Development:
   422→- **Level 1:** The entire defense system/program
   423→- **Level 2:** Major segments (e.g., Platform, Communications, Fire Control, Logistics)
   424→- **Level 3:** Subsystems (e.g., Hardware, Software, Firmware)
   425→- **Level 4:** Components (e.g., Circuit boards, OS modules, crypto modules)
   426→- **Level 5:** Work Packages (WBPs) — discrete deliverable units of work
   427→
   428→### How Defense Companies Manage Cross-Team Collaboration
   429→
   430→#### 1. Program-Centric Organization
   431→Defense companies like EDGE Group typically use a **dedicated program organization** — a cross-functional group providing close management of all project activities and agile decision-making. This includes:
   432→
   433→- **Program Manager** — ultimate accountability for delivery
   434→- **Systems Engineering** — technical authority across all disciplines
   435→- **Integrated Product Teams (IPTs)** — cross-functional teams organized around product segments
   436→
   437→#### 2. Cross-Functional Teams in Complex Product Development
   438→For a product like the KATIM X3M smartphone or KATIM Gateway 9011, the following teams must collaborate:
   439→
   440→| Team | Responsibilities | Collaboration Touchpoints |
   441→|------|-----------------|--------------------------|
   442→| **Hardware Engineering** | PCB design, chassis, antennas, thermal management, display integration | DFM reviews with manufacturing; EMI/EMC testing with firmware |
   443→| **Firmware Engineering** | Bootloaders, secure boot, hardware abstraction layers, device drivers | Hardware bring-up, BSP validation, crypto engine integration |
   444→| **Software Engineering** | OS hardening (Android/KATIM OS), secure apps (Mail, Chat, Voice, Video), middleware | API design with firmware; UX with security team; continuous integration |
   445→| **Cryptography/Security** | Crypto algorithm implementation, key management, threat modeling, penetration testing | Code reviews with all dev teams; certification (FIPS) evidence collection |
   446→| **Testing & QA** | Unit testing, integration testing, system testing, field testing, certification | Requirements traceability with systems engineering; defect tracking with all teams |
   447→| **Manufacturing** | SMT lines, assembly, supply chain management, quality control | DFM/DFA with hardware; production test firmware with firmware team |
   448→| **Regulatory/Compliance** | FIPS 140-2, Common Criteria, MIL-STD certifications | Evidence gathering from all engineering teams; certification body liaison |
   449→| **Systems Engineering** | Requirements management, interface control, architecture, trade studies | Technical baseline management across all disciplines |
   450→| **Program Management** | Schedule, budget, risk management, customer relationships | Status reporting, milestone reviews, change control across all teams |
   451→
   452→#### 3. Work Breakdown Packages (WBPs)
   453→A **Work Breakdown Package (WBP)** is the lowest-level element in a WBS that:
   454→- Has a **defined scope of work** and **clear deliverable(s)**
   455→- Has an assigned **responsible team or individual**
   456→- Has a **budget and schedule**
   457→- Produces a **measurable outcome** (document, code module, hardware prototype, test report)
   458→
   459→Example WBPs for a KATIM X3M development:
   460→- WBP 3.1.1: "Design secure boot chain firmware" (Firmware team, 4 weeks)
   461→- WBP 3.2.3: "Implement end-to-end encryption for KATIM Connect" (Software team, 8 weeks)
   462→- WBP 4.1.1: "FIPS 140-2 Level 4 certification evidence package" (Security team, 12 weeks)
   463→- WBP 5.1.2: "Pilot production run of 500 units" (Manufacturing team, 6 weeks)
